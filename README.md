## Engineering interests

- Traceable AI workflows: document locations, retrieved evidence, and final citations.
- Retrieval and evaluation: separate retrieval metrics, answer correctness, and fixed-reference coverage.
- Practical implementation: Python, LangGraph, LangChain, HTTPX, NumPy, and Streamlit.

I am interested in AI application and RAG engineering roles. The repositories above are the main starting points for reviewing my work.
